Privacy Policy

Your device is the vault. EcoDisassemble AR is a privacy-first application.

1. Local Data (On-Device)

All images, video clips, user-entered symptoms, and local repair history are stored securely on your device using IndexedDB/OPFS. This data is never transmitted to any external server without your explicit action.

2. Edge-AI Processing

We utilize ONNX Runtime Web to perform image and video analysis locally on your device's hardware. Your photos are not sent to a cloud API for inference.

3. Cloud Synchronization (Opt-In)

If you choose to sync data for inventory telemetry, the payload is strictly limited to:

  • part_name (String)
  • quantity (Integer)
  • hazard_flag (Boolean)

We do not transmit user identifiers, location data, or visual media.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

We use essential local storage to remember your privacy choices and triage history. We do not use third-party tracking cookies.
